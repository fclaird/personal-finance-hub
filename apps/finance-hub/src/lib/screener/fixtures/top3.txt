Appendix: China / Taiwan rating notes
High  Apple (AAPL). Greater China (mainland, Hong Kong, Taiwan) was $82.4B, or 17% of FY2026 sales ($478.8B). Substantially all hardware is made by partners primarily in mainland China, Taiwan, India, Japan, South Korea, and Vietnam.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000026/aapl-20260926.htm
High  NVIDIA (NVDA). Wafers come mainly from TSMC (and Samsung). FY2026 revenue: China incl. Hong Kong $19.7B (9%) and Taiwan-headquartered customers $42.3B (20%) of $215.9B.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm
Medium  Microsoft (MSFT). FY2026 revenue was 51% U.S. ($170.8B) and 49% other countries ($161.0B); China is not broken out. Rating is Medium because nearly half of sales are abroad, not because a China share is disclosed.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm
Low  Alphabet (GOOGL). FY2025 revenue was 48% U.S., 29% EMEA, 17% Asia-Pacific, 6% other Americas. China is not a disclosed market; core consumer services are largely unavailable there. All share classes counted once.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000018/goog-20251231.htm
Low  Meta Platforms (META). FY2025 revenue: U.S. $74.8B of $201.0B; Asia-Pacific $53.8B (27%). China is not a disclosed segment; Facebook and Instagram are not generally available there.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000162828026003942/meta-20251231.htm
Low  Netflix (NFLX). FY2025 streaming revenue: U.S. and Canada $20.0B, EMEA $14.5B, Latin America $5.4B, Asia-Pacific $5.4B (12%) of $45.2B. The service is not generally available in China.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065280/000106528026000034/nflx-20251231.htm
Low  Amazon (AMZN). FY2025 sales: North America $426.3B (59%), International $161.9B (23%), AWS $128.7B (18%). China is not broken out.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000004/amzn-20251231.htm
High  Tesla (TSLA). China was $21.0B, or 22%, of FY2025 revenue ($94.8B), and Tesla builds cars in Shanghai.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm
Medium  Home Depot (HD). Only a small share of sales is international, but the 10-K says it kept sourcing offices in China, Taiwan, Vietnam, India, Mexico, and Canada in fiscal 2025.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/354950/000162828026019436/hd-20260201.htm
Low  Walmart (WMT). Walmart U.S. was 68% of FY2026 sales; International was $130.4B (19%) across 18 countries including China. China is not broken out as a material stand-alone line.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/104169/000010416926000055/wmt-20260131.htm
Low  Costco (COST). Operates warehouses in the U.S. and many other countries, including Taiwan and China, but the U.S. is the core of the business. China/Taiwan sales are not broken out.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983226000093/cost-20260830.htm
Medium  Coca-Cola (KO). Swire Coca-Cola, a major bottler, operates in 11 mainland China provinces, Hong Kong, and Taiwan. Coca-Cola does not break out a China revenue percentage.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/21344/000162828026010047/ko-20251231.htm
Low  Eli Lilly (LLY). China was $1.95B, or 3%, of FY2025 revenue ($65.2B).  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000013/lly-20251231.htm
Medium  Johnson & Johnson (JNJ). About 43% of FY2025 sales were outside the U.S., including 15% in Asia-Pacific and Africa. China is not broken out.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm
Low  AbbVie (ABBV). FY2025 10-K does not present China as a 10%+ market. Midgard's compiled 10-K table shows about 2% China; that percentage is unverified here (single compiled source).  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1551152/000155115226000008/abbv-20251231.htm
Low  Berkshire Hathaway (BRK-B). FY2025 10-K has no material China revenue line. Operations are mainly U.S. insurance, rail, and energy. Portfolio holdings (including Apple) are indirect and are not treated as the company's own China sales. Combined Class A and B.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067983/000119312526083899/brka-20251231.htm
Low  JPMorgan Chase (JPM). FY2025 10-K does not break out a material China revenue share. The only Taiwan mention in the extract is about fund share classes, not operations.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000162828026008131/jpm-20251231.htm
Low  Visa (V). U.S. was about 38% of FY2026 net revenue; no other country was 10% or more. The 10-K names UnionPay as the leading network in China.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316126000134/v-20260930.htm
Medium  Exxon Mobil (XOM). FY2025 10-K shows material Asia production (consolidated crude 445 kbd) and a global shipping/marketing system. The filing does not name the Strait of Hormuz.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/34088/000003408826000045/xom-20251231.htm
Medium  Chevron (CVX). FY2025 10-K reports substantial proved reserves and production in Asia and other international areas. The filing does not name the Strait of Hormuz.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/93410/000009341026000078/cvx-20251231.htm
Medium  ConocoPhillips (COP). Produces in the U.S., Norway, Canada, Australia, Malaysia, Libya, China, Qatar, and Equatorial Guinea. Asia Pacific was 4% of 2025 liquids production and 2% of natural gas.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1163165/000116316526000009/cop-20251231.htm
Medium  GE Aerospace (GE). Defense & Systems revenue was $6.57B in 2025, a minority of the company versus commercial engines and services. Global aviation supply and Asia sales are material, but this is not primarily a defense name.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/40545/000004054526000008/ge-20251231.htm
Medium  Caterpillar (CAT). FY2025 external sales were $32.9B inside the U.S. and $34.7B outside (51% international). Asia/Pacific is a reported region; China is not broken out.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/18230/000001823026000008/cat-20251231.htm
Beneficiary  RTX (RTX). A U.S. defense prime. The 10-K discusses Chinese sanctions on the Raytheon business over foreign military sales to Taiwan.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101829/000010182926000006/rtx-20251231.htm
Medium  Linde (LIN). Operates in about 15 Asian and South Pacific countries, including China. A compiled 10-K table (Midgard) puts China at about 8%; that exact share is unverified here. Incorporated in the U.K.; included because it is in the S&P 500.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1707925/000162828026011430/lin-20251231.htm
Low  Newmont (NEM). FY2025 10-K discusses China as a factor in gold and metal demand, not as a disclosed revenue geography. Mines are in the Americas, Australia, and Africa.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1164727/000116472726000010/nem-20251231.htm
Medium  Freeport-McMoRan (FCX). The 10-K says China is currently the largest consumer of refined copper and that U.S.-China tension can affect demand. Operations are in Indonesia, the U.S., and South America.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/831259/000083125926000012/fcx-20251231.htm
Low  NextEra Energy (NEE). FY2025 10-K is focused on Florida Power & Light and U.S. clean-energy generation. No China operations are disclosed.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/753308/000075330826000015/nee-20251231.htm
Low  Southern Company (SO). A U.S. regulated utility. FY2025 10-K does not disclose China operations.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/92122/000009212226000006/so-20251231.htm
Low  Duke Energy (DUK). A U.S. regulated utility. FY2025 10-K does not disclose China operations.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326160/000132616026000014/duk-20251231.htm
Low  Welltower (WELL). FY2025 10-K describes U.S., U.K., and Canadian healthcare properties. China is not a disclosed market.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/766704/000076670426000010/well-20251231.htm
Medium  Prologis (PLD). A global logistics landlord with a material Asia portfolio in addition to the Americas and Europe. A precise China percentage was not extracted from the 10-K text used here, so the Asia exposure is qualitative.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045609/000119312526051453/pld-20251231.htm
Medium  Equinix (EQIX). A global data-center operator with a disclosed Asia-Pacific footprint in addition to the Americas and EMEA. A precise China revenue share was not extracted from the 10-K text used here, so the Asia exposure is qualitative.  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1101239/000110123926000032/eqix-20251231.htm
